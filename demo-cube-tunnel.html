CUBE TUNNEL

Six stock tickers on six faces. Scroll to fly the figure-8.

▼ SCROLL — OR DRAG THE CUBE ▼
01 — THE PATH

A Lissajous 8

The corridor is a 1:2 Lissajous curve — x = A·sin(θ) against y = B·sin(2θ) — which traces a figure-8. Z marches steadily from far to near, so the 8 stretches into a receding serpentine.

02 — THE FRAME

Flying without clipping

At every sample the tangent is crossed with world-up to build a perpendicular frame. Cubes are pushed out along that frame, spiralling around the flight line — so the camera travels a hollow corridor instead of straight through solid geometry.

03 — THE FACES

Drawn, not downloaded

NVIDIA, SpaceX, Tesla, Apple, Amazon and Micron are painted straight onto a <canvas> and handed to the GPU as a CanvasTexture. No network round-trip, no CORS, no missing textures on a cold load.

04 — THE SCROLL

Eased, not snapped

Scroll position sets a target along the curve; the camera eases toward it each frame. The flight keeps gliding for a beat after the wheel stops, which is what makes the 8 read as a path rather than a jump cut.
